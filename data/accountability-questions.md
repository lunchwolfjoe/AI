# TEXANS FIRST* — Accountability & Request-for-Response Package

**Version 1 — September 1, 2026**

## Use and Editorial Protocol

These are reporting questions, not accusations. Each package should be sent only after the cited underlying records are preserved in the newsroom archive. Recipients should be given a clear deadline and told that responses may be quoted or summarized. If a factual premise is disputed, request the record supporting the correction.

Do not send the entire investigation or speculative ledger. Send only the propositions relevant to the recipient. Preserve the original outgoing message, delivery confirmation, full response, attachments and follow-up correspondence.

Where a question asks whether a record exists, a nonresponse is not proof that the record does not exist. Where a recipient corrects a factual premise, verify the correction independently before revising the story.

## 1 — Office of Governor Greg Abbott

### Core Factual Propositions

On January 27, 2026, Governor Abbott directed covered state agencies and public institutions of higher education to freeze new H-1B petitions absent written Texas Workforce Commission permission, required reporting on current sponsorship, and wrote that taxpayer-funded employment opportunities should be filled by Texans first.

The Governor's July 2025 Texas & India profile reports that during 2015–2024 Indian companies announced 74 projects in Texas, $2.7 billion in capital and more than 8,650 jobs, while Texas companies announced 175 projects in India, $7.6 billion and more than 59,300 jobs.

### Questions

- Does the Governor interpret the January 27 directive to apply in any way to H-1B workers employed by private contractors or subcontractors performing taxpayer-funded work for covered state entities?
- Did the Governor's Office direct DIR, TWC or another agency to add Texas-residency, work-location, H-1B or offshore-delivery reporting requirements to statewide technology contracts after January 27?
- Can the Governor's Office state how many contractor labor hours purchased by Texas in fiscal 2025 or 2026 were performed by Texas residents, outside Texas, or outside the United States?
- What metric does the Governor use to determine whether a taxpayer-funded contractor position has been 'filled by Texans first'?
- Please reconcile the 2024 India-mission estimate of more than 10,300 Texas jobs and the July 2025 profile's more than 8,650 jobs.
- Does the Governor support creation of a public contractor-labor ledger reporting prime vendor, material subcontractor, Texas residency, actual work location, aggregate work-authorization category, hours and dollars?

## 2 — Texas Department of Information Resources

### Core Factual Propositions

Current ITSAC pages state that customers may competitively solicit staffing through requests for resumes or SOWs. Templates reviewed by this investigation ask whether work is remote and include obligations for vendor employees/subcontractors accessing state systems, but the reviewed standard fields do not contain a dedicated worker-level field for citizenship, immigration/work-authorization category or offshore delivery.

### Questions

- Does DIR maintain any statewide dataset identifying the Texas residency or actual physical work location of each worker supplied through ITSAC?
- Does DIR collect or require vendors/agencies to collect the immigration or work-authorization category of ITSAC workers?
- Does DIR require disclosure when ITSAC work is performed outside Texas or outside the United States?
- After Governor Abbott's January 27, 2026 H-1B directive, did DIR issue guidance or amend ITSAC/DBITS contracts concerning contractor or subcontractor workers?
- Would DIR support a statewide, privacy-protective labor ledger showing residency, actual work geography, prime/subcontractor relationship, hours and dollars?

## 3 — Texas Workforce Commission

### Core Factual Propositions

Abbott's directive makes TWC the approval and guidance authority for covered public-entity H-1B petitions. The investigation's working records identify a Digerati Systems Business Analyst 2 assignment for TWC unemployment-insurance data-exchange work, approved in January 2026 with a February 6 purchase order authorizing up to $363,377.

### Questions

- Please confirm the Digerati assignment, purchase-order amount, start date, hours authorized and final amount paid.
- Was the selected Digerati worker a Texas resident when the assignment began? What was the worker's primary physical work location?
- Was the worker employed directly by Digerati or through a subcontractor?
- Does TWC prohibit H-1B workers supplied through staff-augmentation vendors?
- How many requests for written permission to file H-1B petitions has TWC received under the Governor's directive, and how many were approved or denied?

## Response Tracking

For each recipient, record:
- Date/time sent
- Exact recipient/address
- Delivery status
- Deadline
- Extension requested
- Response received
- Documents supplied
- Factual corrections asserted
- Verification status
- Story/report sections affected
- Final publication treatment

**Publication language for nonresponse should be factual and narrow:** "[Recipient] did not respond to questions sent on [date]" or "[Recipient] did not answer the question about X." Do not characterize silence as confirmation.
